Use case: ui-mockup / production 2D game HUD sprite.
Create ONE EMPTY MAGAZINE BACKGROUND for an ammunition counter in a hand-painted dark-fantasy military deckbuilding game.
This is an intentionally stylized CUTAWAY magazine HUD, not a photograph. It needs to show separately overlaid horizontal rifle cartridges, one round per row, filling from bottom to top.
Design: upright tall slender STRAIGHT rifle magazine in worn dark gunmetal steel, muted olive-black edges, narrow pewter edge highlights and tiny worn scratches at the outside. Modest feed-lip silhouette at top, simple thicker floorplate at bottom. Keep the large usable body essentially rectangular. No dramatic curved magazine, no perspective, no tilt. Frontal orthographic flat painted UI art with strong clean outline, coherent with practical worn steel game panels.
The intended FULL sprite aspect is 70:270 (7:27): tall and narrow. It will be exported at 280x1080 pixels. Keep the silhouette complete with a small transparent margin. The body should occupy almost all that rectangle.
CRITICAL INTERIOR: a very large continuous EMPTY dark charcoal-blue rectangular well, perfectly quiet and flat, no visible mechanism. Reserve approximately x=10% to90% of width, y=6.7% to95.6% of height as an empty rectangular field to receive bullets separately. This is a broad display cutaway window but its inside is SOLID OPAQUE very dark charcoal, NOT a transparent hole. Near-straight vertical inner sides, straight horizontal top and bottom boundaries. No rounded corners intruding into the usable rectangular field. Do not divide the well into drawn cells. Ten rows of separate bullet sprites will be placed later by software.
Ribs, seams and wear may exist only on the thin side walls and floorplate, not across the well. Keep the framing narrow so short horizontally pointing rifle cartridges remain visible. No center emblem, labels, serial number, text, numbers, symbols, bullets, cartridge silhouettes, followers, springs or slots inside the dark well. No handle, no gun, no hands, no cloth, no scene, no smoke. No gold ornament.
One blank functional magazine background only. Genuine transparent alpha OUTSIDE the magazine, solid dark interior INSIDE. No shadow outside the silhouette or painted checkerboard. Clean game-ready edges. Prefer a simple useful narrow cutaway HUD over elaborate realism.
