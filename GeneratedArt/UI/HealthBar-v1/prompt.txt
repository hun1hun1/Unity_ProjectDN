Use-case: ui-mockup / production 2D game UI sprite atlas.
Create a cohesive reusable HEALTH BAR component sheet for an existing hand-painted dark fantasy deckbuilding roguelike. The attached soldier image and painted card border are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY (brush texture, chunky ink contours, weathered materials); do not depict characters or cards.
The same HP bar will serve both player and enemy, so it must be neutral, symmetric and faction-independent.
ART DIRECTION: restrained weathered charcoal iron frame, very thin muted antique-brass edge accents, chunky hand-painted bevel highlights, subtle imperfect brushwork, confident dark outlines, dark burgundy empty trough and rich crimson-red health fill with a subtle warm red highlight along its upper edge. Small-scale readability takes priority. No ornate filigree, skulls, hearts, shields, crescent moons, faction emblems, spikes, end medallions, huge jewels, digital/sci-fi neon, or pixel art. Long narrow bar with slightly clipped/beveled corners and small solid endcaps. Must look good at approximately 262 x 29 logical pixels.
OUTPUT IS ONE SPRITE ATLAS WITH EXACTLY THREE SEPARATE HORIZONTAL ROWS on genuinely transparent alpha, NOT a presentation/mockup with a background. Landscape canvas, about 1536 x 1024. Large empty transparent gaps between rows, all three precisely horizontally centered, each with a long-narrow 9:1 shape and matching proportions.
TOP ROW: FRAME ONLY. A single continuous closed thin iron-and-antique-brass rectangular beveled ring. Outside AND the entire large central window are fully transparent alpha. Absolutely NO red fill and NO dark backing in its open center. Frame about 1340 px wide and 150 px high; border about 15 px thick, restrained bevel detail. No glow outside frame.
MIDDLE ROW: EMPTY TRACK ONLY. One dark nearly-black burgundy recessed trough, with slight hand-painted depth, shaped to fit INSIDE the top frame's transparent opening. No outer frame. Approximately 1295 px wide and 105 px high. Fully opaque inside the track, transparent outside.
BOTTOM ROW: HEALTH FILL ONLY. One continuous opaque crimson red bar, rich readable red, subtly brushed with a narrow warm upper highlight and deeper lower edge; no black/metal border, no background, no gaps or divisions, no separate icon. EXACTLY THE SAME outline and dimensions as the middle track, so they can be overlaid without shape mismatch. Transparent outside.
Important: top ring remains an empty transparent ring; middle is dark track; bottom is red fill. These are three functional separated layers of ONE game UI asset, not three complete health bars. No text, labels, letters, numbers, watermark, checkerboard pattern, drop-shadow canvas or screenshot. Keep every component completely inside the image without cropping.
