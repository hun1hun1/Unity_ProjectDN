Slime Idle v1

Existing slime artwork animated with slow breathing: small compression, subtle widening and gentle sway.
The bottom contact area is fixed. No new frames were redrawn by AI.

Preview: slime-idle-preview.gif (transparent, infinite loop).
Lossless animated preview: slime-idle.webp.
PNG sprite sheet: slime-idle-spritesheet.png.
Grid: 6 columns x 4 rows; each cell is 768 x 768 pixels.
Playback order: left to right, then top to bottom.
24 frames; 80 ms per frame (12.5 fps); 1.92 seconds per loop.
Individual PNG frames: frames/slime-idle-00.png through slime-idle-23.png.
Ground baseline: y=720 from top; suggested pivot: (0.5, 0.0625).

Source: ../slime-v1.png, originally created with built-in imagegen. Original source unchanged.
Revision method: render the existing sprite on a gently deforming surface, preserving its painted details.
Maximum compression: 18 pixels; maximum width expansion: 2.5 percent. Lower contact area stays fixed.
validation.json records fixed ground pixels, transparent frame borders, source hash and animation metadata.
No Unity scene, prefab or animation controller was modified. Unity runtime playback was not tested.
